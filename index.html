Workflow

Designed around how lab teams actually record work.

1

Create a project

Assign a research group, owner, description, and project code.

2

Add experiments

Generate IDs under the project and keep descriptions editable inline.

3

Record process steps

Capture equipment, status, recipe, notes, and material usage.

4

Link genealogy

Connect parent steps and raw material batches for downstream traceability.

Traceability

From raw batch to finished cell, the lineage stays visible.

The genealogy view shows step nodes, material nodes, and directed links without forcing the team to leave the experiment detail page.

  • Team-scoped access so each research group sees only its projects
  • Auto-numbered project and experiment IDs such as AFE001AA
  • Process steps with equipment, recipe, status, notes, and material usage
  • Step genealogy that links upstream and downstream samples across experiments
  • Equipment and raw material batch databases for traceable lab records
  • Search across projects, experiments, steps, materials, and teams

Team trial

Use this live site as the shared preview for Atlas.

This deployment is a team-friendly demo page for evaluating the workflow, terminology, and interface direction. The full app remains the Django project in the workspace for local database-backed testing.

Recommended trial path
  1. Review project and experiment naming with the team.
  2. Confirm required fields for process steps and materials.
  3. Validate genealogy terminology against current lab practice.
  4. Use feedback to prioritize the next Django app iteration.
